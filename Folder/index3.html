Hong Kong Fun Facts

Fun Facts

    • Hong Kong translates to "fragrant harbor" in Cantonese, likely named for the sweet incense and cargo traded by early settlers.
    • Hong Kong is actually 263 different islands
    • Hong Kong has thousands of high-rise buildings, easily beating out other major metropolitan skylines.
    • The Opium War turned Hong Kong from a fishing village into a financial juggernaut
    • The flag of Hong Kong symbolizes the connection between China and Hong Kong
    • Victoria Peak offers an amazing view
    • Hong Kong is far greener than New York City
    • Hong Kong has beaches that rival the Caribbean
    • The Tsing Ma Bridge is longer than the Golden Gate Bridge
    • Hong Kong locals speak three languages but write in one
