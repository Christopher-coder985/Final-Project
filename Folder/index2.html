Hong Kong History


Inhabited since the Stone Age, Hong Kong evolved from small fishing villages into a British colony after the 1842 Opium War. It grew into a global financial powerhouse before returning to Chinese sovereignty in 1997 under the "one country, two systems" framework.


Fun Facts

    • Hong Kong translates to "fragrant harbor" in Cantonese, likely named for the sweet incense and cargo traded by early settlers.
    • Hong Kong is actually 263 different islands
    • Hong Kong has thousands of high-rise buildings, easily beating out other major metropolitan skylines.
